# What is the conditionnel?

The conditionnel is the French way of saying "would" or "could". Whenever you'd say "I would..." or "I could..." in English, that's a conditional in French.

A few examples:

- **Je voudrais** un café. ("I would like a coffee.")
- **J'achèterais** une voiture. ("I would buy a car.")
- **Pourrais-tu** m'aider? ("Could you help me?")

# How to form it

The conditional follows one rule, with no exceptions on the endings.

:::subject future stem + imperfect endings :::

The future stem is usually just the infinitive of the verb. For -re verbs you drop the final -e first.

The endings (the same as the imperfect) are: **-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient**.

| Subject | parler | finir | vendre |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| je | parlerais | finirais | vendrais |
| tu | parlerais | finirais | vendrais |
| il/elle | parlerait | finirait | vendrait |
| nous | parlerions | finirions | vendrions |
| vous | parleriez | finiriez | vendriez |
| ils/elles | parleraient | finiraient | vendraient |

/t If you already know how to form the future tense, the conditional uses the exact same stem. Just swap the future endings for the imperfect ones. \

## Irregular stems

A few common verbs use irregular stems. The endings stay the same, but the stem you stick them onto isn't the infinitive. These are the ones worth memorising first:

| Verb | Stem | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| aller | ir- | j'irais |
| avoir | aur- | j'aurais |
| être | ser- | je serais |
| faire | fer- | je ferais |
| pouvoir | pourr- | je pourrais |
| vouloir | voudr- | je voudrais |
| savoir | saur- | je saurais |
| venir | viendr- | je viendrais |
| voir | verr- | je verrais |
| devoir | devr- | je devrais |

/i These are the same stems used for the future tense. Learn them once, use them in both tenses. \

# Common mistakes

- Irregular stems are easy to forget. ||The stem of *aller* is *ir-*, not *aller-*. So *j'irais*, not *j'allerais*.||
- For -re verbs you have to drop the final -e first. ||*Vendre* drops it: *je vendrais*, not *je vendrerais*.||
- Imperfect or conditional? ||*Je parlais* means "I was speaking". *Je parlerais* means "I would speak". The extra -r- is the giveaway.||
